At Velodream Sports, we are committed to getting your cycling gear to you safely and reliably. Whether you are ordering a bicycle, frameset, carbon wheelset, component or cycling apparel, we aim to make the delivery process as smooth and transparent as possible.

This policy explains our shipping coverage, delivery estimates, order tracking and how we handle shipments that are delayed, lost or damaged in transit.

1. Shipping Coverage

We currently offer shipping within India to serviceable PIN codes.

Delivery availability may vary depending on the destination, product dimensions, shipment weight and courier service coverage. For certain remote locations or oversized products, additional delivery time or special shipping arrangements may be required.

For international shipping enquiries, please contact our team before placing your order.

2. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, where applicable, will be displayed during checkout or communicated before the order is confirmed.

The applicable charge may depend on factors such as:

The delivery PIN code.

The weight and dimensions of the package.

The type and value of the product.

The courier service and delivery requirements.

Any free-shipping offer, minimum order value or promotional shipping benefit will be subject to the terms displayed at the time of purchase.

For bicycles, framesets, wheelsets and other bulky or high-value products, shipping arrangements may differ from those for smaller accessories.

3. Order Processing Time

Orders are processed after successful confirmation of payment and verification of the order details.

Processing time may vary depending on product availability, order volume and the nature of the purchase.

Ready-to-ship products: These will generally be processed within the timeframe indicated on the website or communicated by our team.

Special-order products: Items sourced specifically for a customer may require additional procurement time.

Custom-built bicycles: Assembly, component availability, quality checks and final preparation may extend the dispatch timeline.

If an order requires additional processing time, we will endeavour to inform you of the expected schedule.

Please note that order processing time is separate from the courier transit time.

4. Estimated Delivery Timeline

Estimated delivery timelines may be displayed on the product page, during checkout or communicated by our team based on the delivery destination and available courier information.

Actual delivery times can vary depending on the PIN code, courier network, product availability and other operational factors.

Delivery estimates are indicative and are not guaranteed dates. Delays may occur because of weather conditions, transport disruptions, public holidays, regional restrictions, peak-season volumes or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

If your shipment appears delayed, please contact us. We will coordinate with the courier service and make reasonable efforts to help resolve the issue.

5. Courier Partners

Velodream Sports may use established third-party courier and logistics providers based on destination, package dimensions, product value and service availability.

The courier assigned to an order may vary. We reserve the right to select the most suitable shipping partner for safe and efficient delivery.

6. Payment and Order Confirmation

All online orders must be paid for in advance. Velodream Sports does not offer Cash on Delivery (COD) option.

Orders will be processed after successful payment confirmation. If a payment is unsuccessful or remains unconfirmed, dispatch may be delayed until the payment status is resolved.

Please verify your order details, delivery address, contact number and selected product specifications before completing payment.

7. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking details will be shared through the contact information provided at checkout, wherever tracking is available.

You can use the tracking reference to follow the shipment through the relevant courier's tracking facility.

Tracking information may take some time to become active after dispatch. If you do not receive tracking details or are unable to locate your shipment, please contact our support team with your order number.

8. Delivery Address and Customer Availability

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, PIN code and reachable contact number.

Please ensure that someone is available to receive the shipment when delivery is attempted. Incorrect addresses, unavailable recipients, inaccessible locations or unsuccessful delivery attempts may cause delays or additional shipping arrangements.

If a shipment is returned to us because of an incorrect address or repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts, we will contact you to discuss the available options. Any additional shipping charges will be communicated clearly and applied only where appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

9. Lost, Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

We take the safe delivery of cycling products seriously.

If your shipment arrives visibly damaged, appears to have been tampered with, contains missing items or includes a product different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

Where possible, retain the outer packaging, shipping label and product packaging, and share photographs or video of the issue to help us investigate.

If a product is confirmed to have been lost or damaged in transit, or the wrong product has been delivered, Velodream Sports will review the circumstances and arrange an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or refund in accordance with our Refund, Returns & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.

For suspected damage to a carbon frame, wheelset or other safety-critical component, please do not install or ride the product until the issue has been assessed.

10. Delivery of Bicycles and Bulky Products

Bicycles, framesets, wheelsets and other large products may require specialised packaging and handling.

Depending on the product, a bicycle may be delivered partially assembled or in protective packaging. Any assembly or installation requirements will depend on the product and the information provided with the order.

Customers should inspect the product upon receipt and arrange professional assembly or inspection where appropriate before riding.

Please contact us if you need clarification about packaging, assembly requirements or delivery arrangements for a particular product.

11. Changes to Orders and Delivery Details

If you need to change your delivery address or other order details, contact us as soon as possible.

We will make reasonable efforts to accommodate a change before dispatch, but changes may not be possible once the order has been packed, handed over to the courier or entered into the delivery network.

Any change that affects shipping charges or delivery timelines will be communicated before it is confirmed, where practicable.

12. Shipping and Refunds

Shipping arrangements for cancelled orders, approved returns and replacement shipments will be handled in accordance with our Refund, Returns & Cancellation Policy.

The treatment of original shipping charges and return shipping costs will depend on the reason for the request, the terms disclosed at purchase and applicable law.

Please review our returns policy for the conditions and procedure applicable to your order.

13. Contact Us

For shipping questions, delivery updates or assistance with a shipment, please contact:

14. Policy Updates

Velodream Sports may revise this Shipping Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operations, courier arrangements or applicable requirements.

The current version will be published on our website. Any updates will remain subject to applicable law and the terms governing orders already placed.
