At Velodream Sports, we want you to shop with confidence. We understand that cycling products require careful selection, compatibility and fit. This policy explains how return requests, refunds, exchanges and order cancellations are handled for purchases made through our website.

Please read the following terms before placing an order.

1. Return Window

Eligible products may be considered for return if you contact us within 5 calendar days of delivery.

All return requests are subject to product eligibility, inspection and the conditions outlined below. This return window does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

2. Products Eligible for Return

A return request may be considered in the following circumstances:

The product received differs materially from its description on our website.

The product arrives damaged or defective.

The wrong product or variant has been delivered.

Apparel or other eligible products do not fit, subject to the conditions in this policy.

Another return reason is accepted by Velodream Sports after review.

Products reported as damaged, defective, incorrect or materially different from the description will be assessed separately from change-of-mind returns.

3. Products Generally Not Eligible for Change-of-Mind Returns

Due to the specialised nature of cycling products, we generally do not accept returns for change of mind, incorrect selection or ordering mistakes involving:

Bicycles, custom-built bicycles and made-to-order builds.

Framesets, carbon wheels and other specialised components that have been installed, used or altered.

Components ordered with incorrect specifications, measurements or compatibility requirements where the product supplied matches the listing and order.

Products that have been used, assembled, fitted, modified or damaged after delivery.

Apparel that has been worn, washed or is missing its original tags.

Products supplied as final sale, where the restriction was clearly disclosed before purchase and is permitted by applicable law.

Products returned without their original packaging, accessories, documentation or included items.

These exclusions do not apply where a return, replacement or refund is required under applicable law, including for eligible defective, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed products.

Need help choosing a component? Please contact our team before ordering. We can help you check compatibility, sizing and specifications to reduce the risk of ordering the wrong item.

4. Conditions for an Approved Return

For a change-of-mind return to be considered, the product must generally meet all the following conditions:

It is unused and in its original condition.

It is returned with original packaging, tags, accessories and documentation.

The return request is submitted within the applicable return window.

The product is securely packed for return shipment.

Velodream Sports has approved the return and provided return instructions.

We may inspect returned products before approving a refund or exchange. Products that do not meet the applicable conditions may be declined for a change-of-mind return, subject to your statutory rights.

Please do not ship any product back before contacting us and receiving return instructions.

5. How to Request a Return

To initiate a return, contact our customer support team at support@velodreamsports.com with the following details:

Your order number and purchase date.

The product you wish to return.

The reason for the request.

Clear photographs or video showing the product, its packaging and any damage or discrepancy, where relevant.

Our team will review the request and advise you on the next steps, including whether the product needs to be returned for inspection.

Returns sent without prior coordination may experience delays in processing.

6. Return Shipping Charges

For approved change-of-mind returns, the customer will generally be responsible for return shipping costs, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

If the product is confirmed to have arrived damaged, defective, incorrect or materially different from its description, Velodream Sports will review the issue and arrange an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the case.

Customers should use suitable packaging and a trackable shipping service where possible. Velodream Sports cannot be responsible for damage or loss caused by inadequate packaging or an untracked return shipment.

7. Refund Options

Once a return has been approved and the product has passed any required inspection, the available remedy will be communicated to you.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

Refund to the original payment method: Where a refund is approved, it will generally be issued to the original payment method.

Exchange or replacement: Where appropriate and available, a replacement or exchange may be arranged by mutual agreement.

Store credit: Store credit may be offered where suitable and accepted by the customer.

Any deductions from a refund, including disclosed payment processing or shipping charges, will be applied only where applicable, communicated clearly and permitted by law.

8. Refund Processing Time

After the returned product is received and any required inspection is completed, Velodream Sports will communicate the refund decision and initiate the approved refund within 3 business days.

The time taken for the amount to appear in your account will depend on your bank, card issuer or payment service provider.

If a refund is delayed beyond the communicated timeframe, please contact our support team with your order details.

9. Order Cancellation

If you wish to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible with your order number.

Before processing: We will make reasonable efforts to cancel the order before it is processed for dispatch.

After dispatch: Cancellation may no longer be possible. You may need to follow the applicable return process if the product qualifies.

Custom-built or special-order products: Orders may enter procurement, preparation or assembly soon after confirmation. Cancellation eligibility will depend on the stage of processing, the terms disclosed at purchase and applicable law.

If an order is successfully cancelled, any applicable refund will be processed in accordance with the terms communicated to you and applicable law.

10. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Deliveries

Please inspect your delivery when it arrives. If you notice visible transit damage, missing items or a product that does not match your order, contact us promptly and provide photographs or video where possible.

For bicycles, framesets, carbon wheels and other high-value components, please avoid installation or use if you suspect damage or a defect. Contact us so the issue can be assessed safely.

We will review the information and determine the appropriate next steps, which may include inspection, repair, replacement or refund, as applicable.

11. Warranty and Manufacturer Support

Some products may be covered by a manufacturer or brand warranty. Warranty coverage, duration and claim procedures vary by product and manufacturer.

A warranty claim is separate from a change-of-mind return. Where appropriate, Velodream Sports will help guide the customer through the applicable warranty process.

12. Contact Us

For return, refund, exchange or cancellation requests, please contact:

14. Policy Updates

Velodream Sports may update this policy from time to time. The version published on our website will apply subject to applicable law and any terms that govern an order already placed.

We are committed to treating every request fairly and transparently while protecting the quality and integrity of the cycling products we supply.

Thank you for choosing Velodream Sports. Ride with confidence.
