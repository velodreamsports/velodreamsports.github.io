1. Introduction

Velodream Sports Pvt. Ltd. (“Velodream”, “Velodream Sports”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect information when you visit our website, purchase products, contact our team or otherwise interact with our services.

Our business includes cycling products such as bicycles, framesets, carbon wheelsets, components, apparel and accessories, as well as related services that may be offered through our website.

This Policy is intended to reflect the privacy and data protection laws applicable in India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, relevant rules currently in force, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and its rules, as and when their respective provisions apply.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will seek that consent in the prescribed manner.

2. Personal Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Velodream. We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

A. Information you provide directly

When you place an order, register an account, contact us or use our services, we may collect:

Contact information: Name, email address, telephone number and delivery address.

Billing information: Billing address, GST details or other invoicing information where required.

Order information: Products purchased, selected sizes or variants, order history, delivery preferences and transaction references.

Payment-related information: Payment status, transaction reference numbers and other information needed to verify a transaction. Card or banking credentials are generally processed by the relevant payment provider.

Customer support information: Messages, enquiries, complaints, photographs, videos and other information you provide when requesting assistance.

Product and service preferences: Information you share about component compatibility, bicycle specifications, sizing or custom-build requirements.

Marketing preferences: Your choices concerning promotional emails, messages and other communications.

Reviews and feedback: Product reviews, testimonials, survey responses and other feedback you choose to submit.

Please avoid sending sensitive financial credentials, passwords or unnecessary identity documents through ordinary email or messaging channels.

B. Information collected automatically

When you browse our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the website configuration and your privacy choices. This may include:

Internet Protocol (IP) address.

Browser type, operating system and device information.

Pages visited, products viewed and links clicked.

Referring website or source.

Date, time and duration of website visits.

Cookie identifiers and similar technical data.

Website performance, error logs and security-related information.

We use this information, where permitted, to operate the website, understand usage patterns, improve the customer experience and protect against fraud or security threats.

C. Information received from service providers

We may receive relevant information from payment gateways, courier companies, ecommerce platforms, analytics providers and other service partners where necessary to process orders, confirm payments, arrange deliveries, prevent fraud or provide customer support.

Where applicable, the processing and sharing of such information will be subject to the relevant legal requirements and contractual arrangements.

3. How We Use Your Information

Velodream may use personal information for the following purposes:

Order processing and fulfilment

Confirming and processing orders.

Verifying payments and issuing invoices.

Arranging dispatch, shipping and delivery.

Communicating order status and delivery updates.

Handling returns, refunds, exchanges and cancellations.

Customer service

Responding to enquiries and complaints.

Providing product information and compatibility guidance.

Assisting with warranty claims and manufacturer support.

Resolving disputes and addressing customer concerns.

Website operation and improvement

Maintaining and improving website functionality.

Understanding which products and pages customers find useful.

Diagnosing technical issues and improving website performance.

Personalising certain website features where permitted.

Security and fraud prevention

Verifying transactions and identifying suspicious activity.

Protecting our customers, website, systems and business.

Investigating suspected fraud, abuse or unauthorised access.

Legal and regulatory compliance

Maintaining business and accounting records.

Meeting tax, invoicing, consumer protection and other legal obligations.

Responding to lawful requests from competent authorities.

Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims where appropriate.

Marketing and promotions

Sending product updates, offers, new-launch announcements and other promotional communications where permitted.

Measuring the effectiveness of marketing campaigns where appropriate.

Providing advertising that may be relevant to your interests, subject to applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected, except where permitted by applicable law.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Depending on the applicable law and the purpose of processing, Velodream may process personal information on the basis of consent, steps requested by you in connection with a purchase or service, compliance with legal obligations, or another legally recognised basis.

Where consent is required, we will seek it through an appropriate notice or mechanism that explains the purpose of collection and use.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent through the relevant mechanism or by contacting us, subject to applicable law.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. It may also affect our ability to provide a particular optional service.

We will not make promotional consent a condition of purchase where such consent is not necessary for the transaction.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies to support essential functions and, where enabled, analytics and advertising.

These technologies may be used for:

Essential functionality: Supporting shopping-cart operations, checkout, account security and other core website features.

Performance and analytics: Understanding website traffic, browsing patterns and technical performance.

Preferences: Remembering settings or choices where supported.

Advertising: Measuring campaigns or delivering relevant advertisements where permitted.

Some cookies may be placed by third-party providers whose services are integrated into our website.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings and any cookie-preference tools provided on our website. Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain the necessary consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties as a business practice.

We may share relevant information with the following categories of recipients when necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

Ecommerce and website service providers: To host, maintain and operate our online store.

Payment service providers: To process transactions, verify payment status, handle refunds and prevent payment fraud.

Courier and logistics partners: To deliver products, provide tracking updates and resolve shipping issues.

Technology and analytics providers: To maintain our website, diagnose technical issues and measure performance, where applicable.

Manufacturers, distributors and warranty partners: To support product enquiries, warranty claims or after-sales service where sharing is necessary.

Professional advisers: Lawyers, accountants, auditors and other advisers where appropriate.

Government authorities and regulators: Where disclosure is required by law, a lawful order or a valid regulatory request.

Business transactions: In connection with a merger, restructuring, acquisition or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We seek to limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Where appropriate, service providers will be required to protect the information and use it only for authorised purposes.

7. Third-Party Services and Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or integrate services operated by third parties, including payment gateways, courier tracking services, social media platforms and manufacturers.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices. We encourage you to review those policies before providing information directly to them.

Velodream is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external websites that we do not control, subject to any obligations imposed on us by applicable law.

8. International Transfers of Information

Some of our ecommerce, hosting, analytics or other service providers may process information on infrastructure located outside India.

Where personal information is transferred outside India, Velodream will follow the restrictions, conditions and safeguards applicable under Indian law, including any relevant government requirements.

We will not assume that a service provider's overseas location removes our obligations concerning personal information.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting, tax, warranty and dispute-resolution requirements.

For example:

Order and invoice records may be retained for applicable accounting, tax and statutory recordkeeping periods.

Customer support records may be retained while an enquiry, warranty matter or complaint is being resolved and for an appropriate period afterwards.

Account information may be retained while your account remains active and as required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Marketing preferences may be retained to respect your communication choices and maintain appropriate suppression records.

Technical and security logs may be retained for a period appropriate to security, diagnostics and legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or render it non-identifiable, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

A request to delete information may not result in immediate deletion of records that we are legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted to retain.

10. Security of Personal Information

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

Depending on the systems and services used, these measures may include access controls, secure connections, restricted administrative access, service-provider safeguards and monitoring for suspicious activity.

Payments are handled through the relevant payment service provider. Customers should use secure devices and avoid sharing account passwords or payment credentials.

No website, network or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We will respond to suspected personal-data incidents in accordance with the requirements applicable to us under Indian law.

11. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to the law in force and the provisions that have commenced, you may have rights or choices relating to your personal information, including:

Requesting information about personal data processed by us.

Requesting correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Requesting deletion of personal information where legally available.

Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

Opting out of promotional communications.

Raising a grievance concerning the handling of your personal information.

Nominating another individual to exercise applicable rights in circumstances permitted by law.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details in the Contact section below. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records.

We will respond within the time limits and through the processes required by applicable law. We may retain information where retention is required or otherwise permitted by law.

Marketing communications

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails by using the unsubscribe link where available. For promotional messages sent through other channels, you may contact us to request that they stop.

Even after opting out of marketing, you may continue to receive essential communications relating to an order, payment, delivery, refund, warranty, security issue or legal requirement.

12. Children's Privacy

Our ecommerce services are intended for individuals who can lawfully enter into transactions, or who are using the website under appropriate parental or guardian supervision where permitted by law.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children in circumstances where parental consent or other safeguards are required but have not been obtained.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action under applicable law.

13. Personal Data Breaches

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will assess the incident and take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and mitigate its effects.

Where applicable law requires notification to affected individuals, regulators or other authorities, we will make the required notifications within the prescribed timelines and through the prescribed channels.

14. Grievance Redressal

If you have a question, concern or complaint about our privacy practices, please contact our designated privacy or grievance contact using the details below.

We will review the complaint and respond in accordance with the timelines and requirements applicable under Indian law.

If you are dissatisfied with the response, you may pursue any further remedy available to you under applicable law, including approaching the relevant authority where legally available.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, products, service providers, data-handling practices or applicable legal requirements.

The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

Where a change requires fresh notice or consent under applicable law, we will take the necessary steps.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to remain informed about our privacy practices.
