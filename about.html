At Velodream Sports, we believe that great cycling should not be defined by the logo on a frame or the price on a tag. It should be defined by the quality of the engineering, the experience of the ride, and the confidence that comes from knowing you have made the right choice.

We are here to challenge conventions, discover what is next in cycling, and bring exceptional products to riders who demand more from every kilometre.

From performance road bikes and precision-engineered carbon frames to high-performance wheelsets, components and personalised bike builds, Velodream brings together quality, value and a genuinely rider-first approach.

Because the best bike is not necessarily the most expensive one. It is the one that inspires you to ride further, push harder and dream bigger.

Our Story

Born from curiosity. Built on conviction.

Velodream began with an idea during late 2024.

As the global cycling industry continued to evolve, we saw exciting developments emerging from manufacturers and brands that were challenging established conventions. We began exploring these possibilities, looking beyond familiar names to discover products that combined advanced engineering, compelling design and outstanding value.

One question sparked the journey:

Why should riders have to pay a premium for a brand name when they could experience exceptional quality through a different approach?

We set out to bridge that gap between global innovation and the Indian cycling community.

The vision was simple: discover promising brands, build meaningful relationships with manufacturers, bring their products to India, and make high-quality cycling equipment more accessible without compromising on the standards that matter.

What started as an idea grew into Velodream Sports — a business built on curiosity, careful product selection, personal relationships and a commitment to doing things differently.

We are still at the beginning of that journey, and we intend to keep moving forward.

What We Stand For

Performance. Value. Trust.

Quality without compromise

We look beyond appearances and brand recognition. We value engineering, materials, design, functionality and the details that influence real-world performance. Our goal is to bring riders products that inspire confidence on every ride.

Exceptional value

Premium cycling should not have to come with an unnecessarily premium price tag. By exploring global manufacturers and emerging brands, we aim to offer compelling alternatives that deliver outstanding value for money.

A customer experience that matters

A great product is only part of the journey. Understanding your needs, helping you choose the right equipment, building your bike to your preferences and being available when you need support are equally important to us.

A passion for the ride

We are cyclists first in spirit. We understand the excitement of a new build, the satisfaction of a perfectly executed ride and the relentless pursuit of personal improvement. That perspective shapes the way we work.

What We Do

Everything you need to build your next great ride.

Performance Bicycles and Frames

Explore road bikes and framesets selected for riders who appreciate thoughtful engineering, contemporary design and performance-oriented construction. Whether you are planning your first serious upgrade or building a machine around your ambitions, we help you find the right starting point.

High-Performance Carbon Wheels

Wheels transform the character of a bicycle. From lightweight climbing setups to deeper aerodynamic profiles and specialised configurations, our wheel offerings are designed for riders looking to enhance the performance, feel and appearance of their bikes.

Cycling Components and Accessories

The right components bring a build together. We source products that balance performance, functionality, reliability and value, giving riders greater flexibility when choosing how to equip their bicycles.

Custom Bike Builds

Your bike should reflect the way you ride. We help bring together frames, groupsets, wheels, cockpits and other components into cohesive builds tailored to your preferences, requirements and budget.

From a carefully considered entry into performance cycling to a highly personalised dream build, our approach begins with understanding the rider.

Introducing VELOVE

Our vision. Our own brand. Our next chapter.

In 2025, the Velodream journey took another important step with the birth of VELOVE.

Created from our passion for cycling and our belief in performance-driven value, VELOVE represents our ambition to develop a brand with its own identity, purpose and place in the cycling world.

We began with wheels — products that can meaningfully influence how a bicycle looks, feels and performs. From there, our ambition is to expand into other areas of cycling, guided by the same principles that shaped Velodream from the beginning.

VELOVE is more than a name. It represents our willingness to move beyond simply bringing existing brands to market and begin building something of our own.

A brand shaped by riders, driven by curiosity and inspired by the possibilities of better cycling products.

VELOVE — Created for the love of the ride.

Velodream Racing Collective (VRC)

More Than Products. A Cycling Community.

At Velodream, our relationship with cycling extends beyond the showroom and the products we offer.

Through our association with riders, our involvement in the cycling community and Velodream Racing Collective (VRC), we celebrate the people who make this sport special.

VRC brings together like-minded cyclists united by a shared passion for riding, friendly competition and continuous improvement. We believe cycling is as much about the people you ride with as it is about the distance you cover or the finish line you cross.

Every new rider, every group ride, every personal best and every race-day achievement contributes to the community we want to help grow.

Our ambition is to support a cycling culture in which more people discover the joy of riding, pursue their potential and inspire others along the way.
